# Prompts: Enigma2 Connect

Werkzeug: integriertes Imagegen (keine CLI).

## Vorschlag 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create a professional original logo proposal for "Enigma2 Connect", a Home Assistant integration connecting Enigma2 television receivers with smart home control, sensors, schedules and automation. Landscape branding presentation with an absolutely OPAQUE PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF). All shapes and typography are solid flat ink with crisp edges and high contrast. Large standalone symbol centered in upper half, then the same smaller symbol and horizontal wordmark centered in lower half. Exact and only text: "Enigma2 Connect", with an actual visible word space. Modern readable sans-serif typography, "Enigma2" semibold and "Connect" regular. Spacious margins, polished restrained visual identity suitable for a small integration icon. No glow, no bloom, no luminous effects, no shadows, no gradients, no metallic texture, no 3D, no official third-party logos, no slogan, no other text. Concept 1, E2 connection monogram: original custom geometric E2 symbol with interlocking letter E and numeral 2, a purposeful diagonal negative-space gap making the two halves appear connected. Strong compact almost-square silhouette, easily identifiable at small size. Deep navy for E, solid teal for 2. Text in navy. It should clearly read E2 and not E3 or EZ.
