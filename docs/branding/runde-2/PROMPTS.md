# Enigma2 Connect – Generierungsprompts, Runde 2

Erstellt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Tool. Drei neue Vorschläge nach Verwerfen des E2-Monogramms wegen visueller Nähe zum bestehenden Zeichen von E2 (e2.org). Für diese neue Runde wurde noch keine eigene Ähnlichkeits- oder Markenregisterrecherche durchgeführt.

## Vorschlag 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished original branding concept board for a Home Assistant integration named exactly "Enigma2 Connect". The integration connects Enigma2 TV receivers to home automation, remote controls, programme information and recording timers. Landscape canvas, opaque pure white background. Upper portion: one large standalone logo symbol. Lower portion: a smaller repetition of exactly the same symbol beside the complete wordmark "Enigma2 Connect", with a clear space between the words. Calm precise sans-serif typography, Enigma2 semibold, Connect medium. Spacious professional composition. Crisp solid flat colors and bold simple geometry suitable for an integration icon at small sizes. No gradients, shadows, glow, 3D, texture, mockup objects or additional text. Do not use an E2 monogram, letters fused with a numeral, diagonal split lettering, an official Home Assistant logo, a house roof, a chain-link icon or a wifi icon. Design a coherent custom symbol rather than a collection of clipart. Direction 1: "Signal port". One geometric television screen outline made from a single substantial dark-indigo stroke with smoothly rounded corners. Its right side opens in one deliberate gap; the lower outline continues outward through this gap as a short horizontal coral-orange signal trace ending in a round dot. A short base under the screen evokes a receiver dock. The screen and outbound connection must read as one clear compact memorable silhouette. Asymmetric in a balanced intentional way, no arrows, no play triangle, no tiny details. Dark indigo and coral-orange only; wordmark dark indigo.
