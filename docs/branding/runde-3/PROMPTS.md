## Vorschlag 1

Use case: logo-brand. Design an original, polished visual identity concept for "Enigma2 Connect", a software integration connecting Enigma2 television receivers to Home Assistant. One landscape presentation on opaque pure white: large standalone symbol in upper center and a small version of the same symbol beside the exact wordmark "Enigma2 Connect" below, spacious margins. Typography must be refined, legible and balanced, with an actual visible space between Enigma2 and Connect. Flat solid ink shapes with crisp edges, confident restrained geometry, suitable for a small integration icon. No texture, gradients, neon, glow, shadows, bevels, 3D or mockups. No slogans or other text. Avoid E2 monograms entirely. Also avoid houses, wifi waves, orbits, chain links, network traces ending in balls, tow-hitch shapes, and horizontal stacked channel bars. Do not copy any existing logo. Direction 1, Open C: A bold original letter C symbol built as a single continuous dark forest-green curved band, with a precise rectangular opening at the right. The inner negative space subtly evokes the rectangular proportions of a television display, while the outer silhouette is a soft rounded square, not a circle. Make its two open terminals distinctive with short opposing inward steps, like a precision connector that can mate, but without any dangling lines or separate objects. Highly reduced, calm and premium. Use forest green as the only symbol color. Wordmark in near-black, slightly rounded contemporary humanist sans serif, Enigma2 medium and Connect bold. The logo should feel like a considered industrial design signature.
