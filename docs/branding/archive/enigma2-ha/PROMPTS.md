### Vorschlag 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished branding concept presentation for the software integration named exactly "Enigma2 HA", which connects Enigma2 television receivers running OpenWebif to Home Assistant. This is a logo exploration, not a website screenshot. Landscape canvas, clean pale warm-white background, generous whitespace, sophisticated flat vector-like graphics, precise bold geometry readable as a small integration icon. One large standalone symbol in upper central area, same symbol paired with the exact wordmark "Enigma2 HA" in lower area. Clear modern sans-serif typography. No other text, no slogans, no watermark, no photographic mockups, no gradients, no shadows, no borrowed official logos. Concept 1: A distinctive unified outline combining a house roof with a television screen; inside the screen, a simple broadcast signal motif made of a dot and two short arcs. Friendly rounded geometry, visually balanced, restrained cyan blue and deep navy color combination. The house and TV should feel like a single memorable mark, not multiple cliparts.

## Überarbeitung aller drei Vorschläge

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied branding concept board. Preserve its exact symbol geometry, layout and text "Enigma2 HA". Change only its rendering: replace the whole dark background with solid opaque pure white (#FFFFFF), remove EVERY glow/halo, shadow, luminous bloom and gradient. Render all symbol and letter interiors as fully opaque solid ink with sharp clean edges. Dark parts must be dark navy or charcoal with strong contrast on WHITE. Accent parts keep their existing hue as solid flat color. The result must look like a crisp flat printed vector logo on a WHITE sheet of paper, not a neon light or embossed metallic logo. Opaque white canvas across all negative space including corners. No transparency.
